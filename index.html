Engineering Architecture

How the Straightener Operates

A meticulously over-engineered system designed to solve a problem that generations of Malayalam proverbs declared physically insolvable.

🧠

1. Arduino Nano Sensor Brain

Equipped with proprietary stubbornness-detection firmware. The microcontroller monitors real-time canine posture and computes the exact cosine curvature required to counteract centuries of spiral inertia.

ATmega328P // 16 MHz Clock
🦾

2. MG996R Metal-Gear Servo

Standard plastic gears will shatter against a dog's proud attitude. Our brass metal-gear drive outputs up to 11 kg·cm of stall torque to gently yet decisively hold the tail in 180° geometric alignment.

Stall Torque: 11 kg·cm @ 6.0V
📐

3. 3D-Printed Caliper Linkage

A dual-opposed parallel linkage mechanism clad in medical-grade silicone pads ensures 100% comfort and zero doggo discomfort while mechanically outsmarting the Fibonacci curl.

PETG 40% Infill // Soft TPU Pads
📜
